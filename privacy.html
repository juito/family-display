Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Family display is a private app used only by the Hart household on a display in their home.

What it accesses

With your permission, the app uses the Google Calendar API (scope calendar.events) to read, add, change and delete events on one family calendar chosen by the household. It does not access any other calendar, email, contacts or files.

Where data is kept

Calendar data and the sign-in token are stored only on the family's own display device at home. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google API data

Family display's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect the calendar in the display's settings, or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token from the display.
